# IG Advisors Tariff Recovery and Benefits Website

## Summary

IG Advisors / IG Biz Advisors is an ImpactWorks project for a conversion-focused advisory website around tariff recovery, advance capital, employee benefits, and Section 125/FICA optimization.

## Portfolio-safe version

Use this for public-facing portfolio/proposal language:

> Developed the site architecture and conversion copy for IG Advisors, positioning two complex B2B financial programs, tariff recovery and Section 125/FICA benefits optimization, into a clear homepage, service pages, and consultation funnel.

## Confirmed components

- Homepage copy and structure.
- Tariff recovery service page.
- Employee benefits and FICA optimization service page.
- About page.
- Conversion-focused CTAs:
  - Book a Free Assessment.
  - See Our Programs.
  - Book a Free Confidential Assessment.
  - Book My Free Consultation.
- Messaging around:
  - Overpaid tariff recovery.
  - Advance capital on qualified claims.
  - Section 125 payroll optimization.
  - Employee benefits funded by FICA savings.
  - Zero-net-cost positioning.
  - High-touch advisory and reporting.
- Separate request for a Section 125 landing page designed for ad conversion.

## Platform / tech

- WordPress.
- Elementor.
- Conversion landing page / sales page structure.
