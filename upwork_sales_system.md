# ImpactWorks Upwork Sales System

## Positioning

**Primary position:** Fractional CMO + AI strategist + implementation team.

**Core promise:** Turn growth and operational bottlenecks into working AI automations, websites, CRM workflows, and customer-facing systems.

**Default entry offer:** ImpactWorks AuditFlow - an AI Automation Audit and Roadmap that identifies, scores, and prioritizes the best automation opportunities before implementation begins.

## Ideal Clients

Prioritize founder-led small and midsize businesses that have real operating complexity and enough urgency to implement:

- Multi-location and franchise businesses
- Professional-service firms with manual lead, proposal, onboarding, or reporting workflows
- E-commerce and medical-product businesses with complex customer or pricing workflows
- Agencies and internal marketing teams that need an experienced implementation partner
- Companies already using ClickUp, PandaDoc, Airtable, HubSpot, WordPress, Shopify, WooCommerce, Make, Zapier, n8n, Twilio, or OpenAI

Avoid generic low-budget data-entry, prompt-writing, and "build my entire AI SaaS" listings with unclear scope.

## Personalized Proposal Loom Script

Target length: 90 seconds to 2 minutes.

1. **Open:** "Hi {{client_name}}, I'm Dante. I reviewed your post about {{job_title}}, and here is how I would approach it."
2. **Show understanding:** Identify two specific requirements, edge cases, or risks from the job post.
3. **Show the plan:** Present a simple map: current trigger -> processing and decisions -> output -> monitoring and exception handling.
4. **Add proof:** Reference the most relevant published portfolio example.
5. **Close:** "If this direction fits, I can turn it into a firm scope, timeline, and milestones after a short call."

Do not build production work for free. A diagram, audit observation, or implementation outline is enough to demonstrate expertise.

## Application Operating Rhythm

- Review jobs twice daily.
- Apply only when the job strongly matches a published case study or core offer.
- Send 3-5 high-quality proposals per day once Connects are available.
- Record Looms only for the highest-value, highest-fit opportunities.
- Track job, client, budget, proposal angle, portfolio proof, response, call, outcome, and objection.
- Review results every 20 proposals and adjust targeting or messaging based on evidence.
