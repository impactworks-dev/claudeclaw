The AI at the Center
Nikki runs
the whole thing.

Nikki is the AI assistant that powers Mission Control. She doesn't just answer questions — she reads and writes across every connected system, builds and deploys pages, manages tasks, and sends Telegram messages in real time.

📱
Telegram Interface
Dante messages Nikki directly on Telegram. Voice messages, commands, questions — Nikki acts on all of it in real time. No app switching required.
🔗
MCP Connectors
Nikki has live connections to Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, ClickUp, Vendasta, Notion, Make, Supabase, Canva, Figma, Gamma, Vercel, and more.
⏰
Scheduled Tasks
Recurring tasks run automatically on cron schedules — morning briefs, pipeline syncs, email digests — all queued and executed without Dante lifting a finger.
🤖
Multi-Agent System
Nikki can delegate to specialist agents — research, comms, content, ops — for heavy parallel tasks. Each agent is picked up and executed within 60 seconds.
🧠
Persistent Memory
Nikki remembers everything across sessions — client context, project state, preferences, and business facts — stored in SQLite and recalled automatically.
🏗️
Builds & Deploys
Nikki writes HTML, deploys to Fly.io, and publishes pages to Mission Control instantly. This very page was built by Nikki in a single session.
