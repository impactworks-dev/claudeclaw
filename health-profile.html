⚠️ Medication Interaction Notes

Vyvanse + Strattera: Both increase norepinephrine. Can amplify cardiovascular effects (elevated HR, higher BP). Most significant combo given stroke history. Prescribing team likely monitoring intentionally.
Baclofen + Vyvanse: CNS depressant vs stimulant. Partially counteract each other. Not dangerous but may reduce perceived effectiveness of either.
Aspirin + stimulants: Stimulants raise HR/BP, aspirin is protective against clotting. Smart pairing for stroke risk.
Atorvastatin: No significant interactions with any other med on the list. Different metabolic pathways.
Regular BP and heart rate checks recommended due to Vyvanse + Strattera overlap.
