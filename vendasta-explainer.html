Account Management
Accounts, contacts,
deals — all in one view.

Vendasta keeps a clean record of every business relationship. Accounts are companies. Contacts are the people. Deals are active opportunities tied to both.

🏢
Accounts
Every business we're working with or pursuing. Synced to ClickUp for delivery once they become active clients. Source of truth for all company data.
👤
Contacts
The individual humans at each account — decision makers, project leads, billing contacts. Tied to accounts with role and relationship context.
💼
Deals
Active opportunities with value, stage, and expected close date. When a deal closes, it triggers the ClickUp handoff for project delivery.
🤖
Nikki's Access
Nikki reads and writes to Vendasta via the custom MCP connector (namespace 0BYD). Can query accounts, update stages, and check pipeline status on demand.
